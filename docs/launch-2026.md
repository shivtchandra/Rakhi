## Ten short-video scripts

Show the box opening in the first two seconds. Keep each video under 15 seconds and end on the relevant landing page.

1. **Courier cutoff:** Reveal first. “Missed the courier cutoff? I made a Rakhi that arrives on WhatsApp.” Show three creator taps. End: “Free. No signup.”
2. **Not another forward:** Reveal first. “Please do not send another forwarded Happy Rakhi image.” Show recipient name and custom message.
3. **Brother abroad:** Reveal first. “When your brother is abroad but you still want him to open something.” Show the long-distance page.
4. **Sister flow:** Reveal first. “Rakhi is for sibling bonds, so I made one for my sister too.” Show sister selection and send.
5. **Sixty seconds:** Start a timer beside the creator. Choose recipient, Rakhi, message, share. Stop when WhatsApp opens.
6. **Funny sibling:** Reveal a funny message. “For the sibling you love and would still mute in the family group.”
7. **Song:** Reveal with Spotify visible. “I hid our song inside her Rakhi surprise.”
8. **Shagun:** Show acceptance and shagun. “He opened the Rakhi and had no excuse left.”
9. **Made for a real person:** Show a named demo. “I made this for Riya instead of sending her a generic festival text.”
10. **Rakhi morning:** Reveal first. “If you are seeing this on Rakhi morning, you still have time.”

## Community posts

Adapt the first sentence and example to each community. Never paste the same post repeatedly.

1. **Maker story:** “I was late sending a Rakhi, so I built a free one that opens like a gift box. I need honest feedback on whether the creation flow is quick enough.”
2. **Long distance:** “For anyone whose sibling is in another city or country, I made a no-shipping Rakhi link that opens on any phone.”
3. **Student:** “Made this for students who cannot travel home for Rakhi. It is free, and recipients do not need an account.”
4. **Design/build:** “I built a browser-based 3D Rakhi and gift-box reveal. The interesting product question is whether recipients create the next gift.”
5. **Last minute:** “Courier cutoff is over, so I made a personal fallback that feels better than a forwarded greeting image.”

## Stop and continue rules

- Continue a channel after 25 visits if at least 30% start creating.
- Rewrite the post or landing-page match if fewer than 20% start creating.
- Fix the creator flow if starts are healthy but fewer than 40% generate.
- Fix the share screen or message if fewer than 50% of generated gifts produce a share click.
- Scale examples that create recipient-loop starts. Do not scale on impressions alone.
